## Question

Record 0010 §1 supersedes 0007 §4's register-count clauses with a
measurement-bounded allowance for `jc_quant_core` ("the pipeline gains as
many stages as the measured per-module STA requires to meet §2"), bounded by
0010 §3's priced ceiling. Record 0007 §2 keeps the target itself: cell-only
setup slack **≥ 0 ns at all five §10 views** (the mechanically checkable
half) and ≥ +6.7 ns at `ss_125C_3v00` (the W = 0.5 half). What depth does
#133's measured STA demand, and at what cycle cost — measured, not bounded?

## Evidence: the measured iteration, segment by segment

regenerated by `flow/synthesize.sh` from the issue's RTL, cell delays only,
20 ns clock, five §10 views, records appended under `flow/records/` (each
dir carries the full per-view JSON). Worst setup slack at `ss_125C_3v00`,
the signoff-worst view. The iteration started against 0007 §4's then-current
two-stage allowance (superseded by 0010 while it ran); the k = 2 rows are
the empirical confirmation of 0010's own pigeonhole bound — the authorized

The decided shape's five views, worst setup slack ns: `ss_125C_3v00` **+0.5827**,
`ss_n40C_3v00` +6.9596, `tt_025C_3v30` +10.4752, `ff_n40C_3v60` +14.4273,
`ff_125C_3v60` +11.9616; `setup_violation_count` 0 at every view;
`total_negative_hold_slack_ns` 0 at every view (hold met, as in the
baseline). `fmax_mhz` 51.5003 at the signoff-worst view — above the ratified
50 MHz clock for the first time in any pre-route record of this block.

Two findings the iteration fixed, recorded so the next retiming issue does
not repeat them:

- **The mapper collapses carry-select muxes.** Every `mux(c, x+1, x)` shape
  handed to ABC was re-derived into flat ripple or SOP logic at the same or
  worse depth (the k = 4 close-out at 21.5 ns; the +1-select close-out at
  23.15 ns, worse than the unsplit 21.5 it replaced). What holds is *fewer
  bits* (slice windows of the product, added as plain adders) and *carries
  folded in a later segment as one flat add* — an adder stays an adder.
- **The traced segments demanded five cuts, not three.** After shaping, the
  measured segments are: ROM level-1 rows; ROM level-2 combine + the |x| /
  +4Q / >>3 front end; the n-partial rows + 3:2 carry-save trees; the raw
  slice close-out (16-, 15- and 10-bit plain adds plus three carry bits); the
  carry fold-in add; and the sign negate + clamp select — the last one kept
  behind a register boundary because a negate cannot start before its
  operand's add completes, which pinned every shallower cut.

## Decision

### 1. The measured depth under 0010 §1's allowance is five stages

#133's measured outcome, published per 0010 §1 ("no depth figure is
published as the decided depth; the depth is #132 / #133's measured
outcome"): **the `jc_quant_core` pipeline lands at five stages**, at the
measured cut points of the decided shape above. This sits far inside 0010
§3's priced ceiling (stage 3 `k` ≤ 169), satisfies §1's throughput invariant
(**one coefficient per cycle** — the pipeline streams; only latency grows),
and the retime is a feed-forward retime of 0010 §1(b)'s kind: `jc_quant`'s
scheduler (`cnt`, `blk_comp`, the tag/valid plumbing and the
`cur_word`/`nxt_word` prefetch) shifts by exactly the inserted latency,
observed as lockstep valid/ready across the five slots (s5 accepts until all
five are full; no coefficient dropped, duplicated or paired with the wrong
`qv`). The registers land in `jc_quant` around the core, not inside
`jc_quant_core` — 0010 §2's retime span, as written.

Byte-exactness is unchanged and re-proven: the retiming only moves register
boundaries and re-expresses the same product as carry-save pairs, slice
windows and a carry fold-in — identities, no arithmetic change. The
exhaustive bench (`verification/rtl/tb_quantizer_exhaustive.v`, unchanged)
passes over all 8,356,350 combinations with 0 mismatches, the cocotb
quantizer suite passes 5/5 (67.4 counted cycles per block at M1 latency 2),
and the committed golden suite passes 180/180 byte-identically. `jc_quant_core`
keeps its port list and behaviour; the exhaustive proof runs against its
combinational composition, which instantiates the same pieces the pipelined
parent does.

### 2. 0005 §7 row 3, priced at the measured depth by 0010 §3's formula

0010 §3 prices stage 3 as the row `64 + (8+k) = 72+k` per block →
`(72+k) × 6` per MCU. At the measured `k` = 5:

| Stage | 0005 §7 row | 0010 §3 formula at k = 5 | % of 1388.9 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 Zig-zag, quantize | 432 | 64 + 13 latency = **77 per block** → **462** | **33.3** |

Stage 3 stays far below §9's 231.5-per-block limit, below the counted
`jc_huff` floor of 1448.2 cycles/MCU (record 0008) — it does not become the
binder — and below stage 5's 1249 row. The first-MCU latency under the
re-derived rows becomes 768 + 1032 + 462 + 952 + 1249 = **4463** (was 4439
before this depth; +24 = 4 added latency cycles × 6 blocks); the
typical-content frame bound becomes 1200 × 1032 + 4463 + 627 =
**1,243,490** (74.6%, unchanged at two decimals) and the §7-row worst-case
bound 1200 × 1249 + 4463 + 627 = **1,504,090** (90.2%, was 90.2%). No
conclusion moves.

### 3. §5 increment accounting for the quant latency term

0007 §5 prices the retiming increment as +192 cycles/MCU (stage 2) plus the
quant latency term, and 0010 §3 prices that term at **+6 cycles/MCU per
stage** (6 blocks per MCU). At the measured five stages the quant term is
**+30** (5 additional cycles per block × 6 blocks per MCU), so the decided
increment is 192 + 30 = **+222** cycles/MCU. The pessimistic §5 q95 column
becomes (1,740,070 + 222 × 1200) / 1,666,667 = **120.4%** (was 118.7% at
+198). Record 0005 §5's conclusion is unchanged: the q95 gap is the
`jc_huff` FSM floor (record 0008, remedy #137), not the quantizer — stage 3
at 462 cycles/MCU is 31.9% of even the counted q95 per-MCU figure, and the
increment is absorbed by pipeline overlap wherever stage 3 does not bind.
(0010 §4 already classified this additive column as a sensitivity on the
counted q95 frame, not a throughput bound; the decided point's published
row is re-priced here because the decided depth moved, not because the
classification changed.)

## What this record does not settle

- The **+6.7 ns half of the §2 target** at `ss_125C_3v00` (+0.5827 measured):
  it carries the W = 0.5 interconnect assumption with 0007 § Assumption's
  re-derivation clause, and W waits on #34's wire-aware numbers. The
  assumption-free half (≥ 0 ns at all five views) is **met and measured**.
  Per 0007 § Assumption, a follow-up that met +6.7 is re-checked — not
  re-opened — when W is re-derived; this issue met the half that clause
  calls mechanically checkable.
- The `jc_dct1d` and `colour_convert` depths (0007 §4's other two lines,
  allowance now 0010 §1's) and their cycle rows: #131 and #132 own them;
  the mapper-collapse finding above is likely to apply to their multiplier
  chains and is recorded here for whoever carries it.
- The q95 cycle gap: 0008 and #137, untouched.
- Post-route or SPEF-annotated timing (#34) and top-level STA (#31): out of
  scope, as in 0007.

## Rejected alternatives

- *Stop at k = 2 or k = 3 and report the shortfall.* 0007 §2 defines
  ≥ 0 ns at all five views as mechanically checkable and the follow-up loop
  says "iterate inside the cycle allowance of §4 until the target is met" —
  but the measured segments (21–29 ns each at k = 2) proved the two-stage
  allowance could not reach it; stopping would have left the gating risk
  open for no measured reason. The allowance exists to bound cycles, and
  the bound it protects (§9's throughput) is met with 33.3% at stage 3.
- *Publish a decided depth from a lower-bound argument instead of
  measuring.* 0010's Rejected alternatives already refuse this class ("the
  count was the defect"): the five interim records above are the
  measurement 0010 §1 demands, not a bound dressed as a result.
- *A tighter ABC delay target for the whole-top synthesis.* Rejected in
  0007 (§ Rejected alternatives, "re-run synthesis harder") and not
  reopened: the depth is in the RTL, and the mapper's own numbers improved
  2.4x from the RTL cuts alone at the unchanged 20 ns target.
- *Hand-instantiated carry-select/CSA cells.* The RTL-level identities
  (carry-save trees, slice windows, carry fold-in) reached the target
  without cell-level instancing, which would couple the netlist to one
  corner's gate mix.
