# Decision record 0009: the `jc_quant_core` pipeline depth, measured

**Status**: Decided, agent-decidable (2026-11-15, issue #133)
**Resolves**: #133 (the `jc_quant_core` retiming follow-up); discharges the
§4-amendment disposition #144 filed against record 0007
**Spec**: `spec/codec.md` §9 (unchanged), §10 (unchanged); amends

`jc_quant_core` depth line and its re-derived §7 row 3) and the §5 increment

## Question

Record 0007 §4 authorizes `jc_quant_core`'s pipeline at "one stage at the
ROM/multiply boundary, optionally two". Issue #133's measured STA shows that
allowance cannot reach even the assumption-free half of the §2 target
(≥ 0 ns at all five §10 views): at the authorized two-stage depth the
signoff-worst view still misses by 9.95 ns, with every one of the three
resulting segments at 21–29 ns against a ~19.8 ns segment budget. What depth
does the measured evidence authorize, and at what cycle cost?

dir carries the full per-view JSON). Worst setup slack at `ss_125C_3v00`,

The decided shape's five views, worst setup slack ns: `ss_125C_3v00` **+0.5827**,
`ss_n40C_3v00` +6.9596, `tt_025C_3v30` +10.4752, `ff_n40C_3v60` +14.4273,
`ff_125C_3v60` +11.9616; `setup_violation_count` 0 at every view;
`total_negative_hold_slack_ns` 0 at every view (hold met, as in the
baseline). `fmax_mhz` 51.5003 at the signoff-worst view — above the ratified
50 MHz clock for the first time in any pre-route record of this block.

Two findings the iteration fixed, recorded so the next retiming issue does
not repeat them:

- **The mapper collapses carry-select muxes.** Every `mux(c, x+1, x)` shape
  handed to ABC was re-derived into flat ripple or SOP logic at the same or
  worse depth (the k = 4 close-out at 21.5 ns; the +1-select close-out at
  23.15 ns, worse than the unsplit 21.5 it replaced). What holds is *fewer
  bits* (slice windows of the product, added as plain adders) and *carries
  folded in a later segment as one flat add* — an adder stays an adder.
- **The traced segments demanded five cuts, not three.** After shaping, the
  measured segments are: ROM level-1 rows; ROM level-2 combine + the |x| /
  +4Q / >>3 front end; the n-partial rows + 3:2 carry-save trees; the raw
  slice close-out (16-, 15- and 10-bit plain adds plus three carry bits); the
  carry fold-in add; and the sign negate + clamp select — the last one kept
  behind a register boundary because a negate cannot start before its
  operand's add completes, which pinned every shallower cut.

## Decision

### 1. `jc_quant_core`'s pipeline is authorized at five stages

Record 0007 §4's line — "`jc_quant_core`'s pipeline gains one stage at the
ROM/multiply boundary, optionally two" — is amended to: **the pipeline gains
up to five stages**, at the measured cut points of the decided shape above.
This is the depth the measured STA demanded, per the order #144 set ("the
depth comes from a measurement, not from a pre-committed lower-bound
argument"); the four interim records above are that measurement. Throughput
stays **one coefficient per cycle** (the pipeline streams; only latency
grows) — the invariant 0007 §4 binds the depth to. Beyond five stages
requires a new record.

Byte-exactness is unchanged and re-proven: the retiming only moves register
boundaries and re-expresses the same product as carry-save pairs, slice
windows and a carry fold-in — identities, no arithmetic change. The
exhaustive bench (`verification/rtl/tb_quantizer_exhaustive.v`, unchanged)
passes over all 8,356,350 combinations with 0 mismatches, the cocotb
quantizer suite passes 5/5 (67.4 counted cycles per block at M1 latency 2),
and the committed golden suite passes 180/180 byte-identically. `jc_quant_core`
keeps its port list and behaviour; the exhaustive proof runs against its
combinational composition, which instantiates the same pieces the pipelined
parent does.

### 2. Re-derived §7 row 3 under the decided depth

Record 0005 §7's stage-3 row, re-derived by 0007 §4 at 73 per block and
amended here for the five-stage depth:

| Stage | 0005 §7 row | 0007 §4 re-derivation | **This record** | % of 1388.9 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 Zig-zag, quantize | 432 | 73 per block → 438 | 64 + 13 latency = **77 per block** → **462** | **33.3** |

Stage 3 stays far below §9's 231.5-per-block limit, below the counted
`jc_huff` floor of 1448.2 cycles/MCU (record 0008) — it does not become the
binder — and below stage 5's 1249 row. The first-MCU latency under the
re-derived rows becomes 768 + 1032 + 462 + 952 + 1249 = **4463** (0007 §4's
4439 re-derived + 24); the typical-content frame bound becomes
1200 × 1032 + 4463 + 627 = **1,243,490** (74.6%, unchanged at two decimals)
and the §7-row worst-case bound 1200 × 1249 + 4463 + 627 = **1,504,090**
(90.2%, was 90.2%). No conclusion moves.

### 3. §5 increment accounting for the quant latency term

Record 0007 §5 prices the retiming increment as +192 cycles/MCU (stage 2)
plus +6 (quant latency at one stage: one additional cycle per block × 6
blocks per MCU) = +198. At the five-stage depth the quant term is **+30**
(5 additional cycles per block × 6 blocks per MCU), so the increment is
192 + 30 = **+222** cycles/MCU. The pessimistic §5 q95 column becomes
(1,740,070 + 222 × 1200) / 1,666,667 = **120.4%** (was 118.7% at +198).
Record 0005 §5's conclusion is unchanged: the q95 gap is the `jc_huff` FSM
floor (record 0008, remedy #137), not the quantizer — stage 3 at 462
cycles/MCU is 31.9% of even the counted q95 per-MCU figure, and the
increment is absorbed by pipeline overlap wherever stage 3 does not bind.

## What this record does not settle

- The **+6.7 ns half of the §2 target** at `ss_125C_3v00` (+0.5827 measured):
  it carries the W = 0.5 interconnect assumption with 0007 § Assumption's
  re-derivation clause, and W waits on #34's wire-aware numbers. The
  assumption-free half (≥ 0 ns at all five views) is **met and measured**.
  Per 0007 § Assumption, a follow-up that met +6.7 is re-checked — not
  re-opened — when W is re-derived; this issue met the half that clause
  calls mechanically checkable.
- The `jc_dct1d` and `colour_convert` depths (0007 §4's other two lines) and
  their cycle rows: #131 and #132 own them; the mapper-collapse finding
  above is likely to apply to their multiplier chains and is recorded here
  for whoever carries it.
- The q95 cycle gap: 0008 and #137, untouched.
- Post-route or SPEF-annotated timing (#34) and top-level STA (#31): out of
  scope, as in 0007.

## Rejected alternatives

- *Stop at k = 2 or k = 3 and report the shortfall.* 0007 §2 defines
  ≥ 0 ns at all five views as mechanically checkable and the follow-up loop
  says "iterate inside the cycle allowance of §4 until the target is met" —
  but the measured segments (21–29 ns each at k = 2) proved the allowance
  itself cannot reach it; stopping would have left the gating risk open for
  no measured reason. The allowance exists to bound cycles, and the bound it
  protects (§9's throughput) is met with 33.3% at stage 3.
- *A tighter ABC delay target for the whole-top synthesis.* Rejected in
  0007 (§ Rejected alternatives, "re-run synthesis harder") and not
  reopened: the depth is in the RTL, and the mapper's own numbers improved
  2.4x from the RTL cuts alone at the unchanged 20 ns target.
- *Hand-instantiated carry-select/CSA cells.* The RTL-level identities
  (carry-save trees, slice windows, carry fold-in) reached the target
  without cell-level instancing, which would couple the netlist to one
  corner's gate mix.

## Consequences

- `rtl/jc_quant.v` instantiates `jc_quant_rom_rows`, `jc_quant_mul`,
  `jc_quant_sum_windows` and `jc_quant_clamp` directly with the five
  pipeline registers in the parent; `jc_quant_core` stays the combinational
  composition of the same pieces (0005 §2's `jc_<stage>_` file rule; 0007
  §1's authorization).
- `verification/rtl/test_quantizer.py`'s `test_cycles_per_block` asserts the
  **77**-cycle row and cites this record; the logged counted figure is
  67.4 cycles per block at M1 latency 2.
- `flow/records/` carries the seven directories named above (append-only);
  the decided shape's record is `20261115T221343Z-8ecbcd0`.
- #144 closes on this record's evidence: the §4 amendment it asked for is
  landed here, with the measured STA as its basis and the depth priced by
  the one-coefficient-per-cycle invariant (§1) rather than a pre-committed
  count.
- The five ss-only misses of 0007 (other modules, ≤ 4.44 ns) stay out of
  scope, as do post-route numbers (#34), the macro LEF (#31) and P&R.
